University projects / 2022

Ideas, tested with a team

Two case projects gave me room to practice strategy, presentation, and creative problem-solving.

Redseer — LAU Sailors Consulting

From January to March 2022, our team developed a proposed growth strategy for a UAE grocery retailer. It combined customer engagement, sustainability ideas, and digital experiences around Ramadan. This was a case-competition proposal; projected outcomes were not measured business results.

L’Oréal Brandstorm — FREA

From May to June 2022, I worked on a beauty concept centered on sustainability and inclusion. The proposal explored reduced wash-off needs, packaging, and a broad shade range. These were concept features, not a launched product or validated formulation.

Both projects were developed as university competition proposals in 2022.
