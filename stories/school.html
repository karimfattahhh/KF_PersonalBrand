My story · Giving back

From student to speaker.

I went back to Beirut Annunciation Orthodox College, my former school, to speak with Grade 11 students about business, economics, and entrepreneurship.

Back in the classroom

The conversation covered economic models, startup thinking, adaptability, and continuous learning. In my post afterward, I wrote about the curiosity and enthusiasm of the students, and how grateful I was for the welcome from the students and faculty.

A chance to contribute

I thanked Doris Chalhoub and Taymour Haydar for inviting me. I cherished the opportunity to contribute and hoped to inspire others. That is a part of this journey I want to keep making room for: sharing what I’m learning with people who are finding their own starting point.
