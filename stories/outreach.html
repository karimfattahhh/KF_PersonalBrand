Studypedia & Docubid / September—December 2022

Trust, turned into participation

Campus relationships became a practical education in outreach and service.

Studypedia

As a brand ambassador, I promoted study-abroad opportunities and helped bring more than fifty students to the second International Educational Fair in October 2022. Personal conversations and a network built at LAU were central to that work.

Docubid

My work included brand outreach, referrals, and tutoring. As the availability of my own instructor network changed, these connections gave students another route to support.

What it taught me

Knowing people is only the beginning. They need to understand what you are offering, why it might help, and whether they can trust you to follow through.
