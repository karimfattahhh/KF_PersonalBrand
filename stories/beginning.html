My story · My turning point

I wasn’t always the strong student.

When I wanted to enter the Lebanese American University, the question wasn’t only how we would afford it. I also felt the doubt around whether I was academically good enough to belong there.

My personal story

The starting point

I wasn’t doing particularly well academically at school. LAU was expensive, and people around me raised concerns about whether it was financially possible. Alongside that, I felt an expectation that a university like LAU might be beyond what I could do. Those weren’t necessarily the words people used, but that was how it landed with me.

Taking the challenge

I took that as a challenge. Entering university was a turning point: a chance to build beyond the picture I had of myself as a student. Consistency, dedication, and persistence are values I want to carry into each new chapter.

Where that chapter led

I graduated from LAU with distinction, as an Honors Program graduate and a MEPI TLS scholar. Those milestones matter to me because of where I started and the doubts I carried into that experience.

Still becoming

I’m still building myself. University is one chapter of that process; the work, teaching, and learning I’m doing now are the next. If you’re questioning what you’re capable of, I hope this part of my story gives you a reason to keep exploring it.
