House of Prep

From Test Prep to a broader home for learning.

Where I joined the story

I was already tutoring with Test Prep Institute when I joined as Business Development Manager in August 2025. Over the following year, we worked to develop House of Prep: a shared home for several learning needs and stages.

One home, different needs

  • TestPrepStandardized-test preparation.
  • PrepMeSubject courses connected to the school curriculum.
  • TalebUniversity learning.
  • WeGuideCareer guidance and counseling.
  • ElmyThe technology foundation supporting the wider ecosystem.

What I bring to the work

My work connects business development, brand structure, communication, and the learner experience. That includes making the relationships between services clearer, developing digital experiences and planning materials, and helping turn an ambitious idea into practical work.

Why it matters to me

I know what it feels like to struggle as a learner, and what changes when someone finds an explanation that works. I want that understanding to shape the businesses and tools we build, as well as the way I teach.

Still in progress

This is ongoing team work, developed through disruption in Lebanon. The shared direction is larger than a set of brand names: a place where people can find guidance, support, and a next step.

An ongoing team effort, bringing together the different stages of a learner’s journey.
