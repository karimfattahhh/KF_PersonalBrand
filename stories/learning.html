My story · Teaching & confidence

Everyone can learn.

At the end of a teaching semester, I found myself reflecting on clarity, confidence, and the capacity to think critically.

FOCUS

Teaching at TestPrep

CONTEXT

Adapted from a LinkedIn post

Clarity over complexity

Whether we were breaking down economic models or exploring business frameworks and strategies, the goal was to help students understand. In my post, I thanked the students who showed up, stayed curious, asked questions, and trusted me with their learning.

Learning how to learn

I wrote that there is no such thing as “not smart.” Everyone can learn. The key is learning how to learn and identifying an approach that works for you. That belief is central to the kind of teacher I want to be.

A reminder I can use too

As I keep building my own skills, this is a useful principle to carry with me: when understanding doesn’t come immediately, there may be another way into the problem.
