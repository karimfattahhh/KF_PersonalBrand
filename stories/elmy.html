The challenge

A learning-intelligence platform has to communicate with people who bring different questions: educators, school leaders, students, and partners. Its value needs to be understandable before its features can be useful.

The work

My involvement has focused on shaping the website narrative, directing visual explanations, and documenting the operational work behind assessment delivery. This includes turning complex product information into clearer pages and bringing preparation, check-in, wayfinding, and delivery into a visual story.

A clearer experience

The website project combines a shared visual system, product explanations, and a methodology film. The emphasis is on helping visitors understand the platform and find a relevant next step.

Elmy is an independent learning-intelligence platform. This website redesign is currently in review.
