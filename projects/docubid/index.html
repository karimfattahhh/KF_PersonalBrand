Demand beyond one person

Tutoring began as something I could do myself, then grew into a network of instructors. Eventually, demand and the changing availability of that team made it difficult to handle every request within the same setup. Docubid became one of the ways I could connect students with additional support.

I referred students to the platform and also tutored through it. On LinkedIn, I describe the role as Writer and Brand Ambassador: a combination of academic support, written content, client communication, and outreach.

02

Explaining the service

My ambassador work involved promoting Docubid’s services through personalized communication and community engagement. It built on the relationships I had developed as a tutor and the trust students placed in me when they asked where to find help.

My LinkedIn account records onboarding more than fifty clients within one month. The important part of that experience for me was the connection between communication and action: understanding a request, explaining the available support, and helping someone take a next step.

A note I’m carrying forward

A network grows through relationships. Its value depends on what happens after the introduction.

03

The work behind the referral

The role included writing and editing across academic subjects, alongside tutoring. Those responsibilities called on different aspects of communication: organizing ideas on a page, adapting an explanation, and responding to what a person was struggling to understand.

In tutoring, I worked to simplify complex material and approach a concept from more than one angle. That had already become central to how I learned myself. Explaining something clearly meant first recognizing where the difficulty was for the person in front of me.

04

A small piece of the outreach

The promotional card saved with this project on LinkedIn carries my KF20 code. It is a concrete piece of the ambassador work—a small artifact from a role that otherwise happened largely through conversations, referrals, and support.

It also captures the overlap I was beginning to understand between education and business. Helping people find a service involves communication and relationships; delivering the support requires care and follow-through. Both sides influence whether that relationship continues.

05

What the experience added

Docubid gave me further practice in client relations, written communication, and explaining services. It also showed me how a teaching network could connect with a wider organization when one person or one small team could not handle everything.

The experience sits within a longer thread in my work: learning something, making it understandable, and finding ways to connect people with the help they need.
