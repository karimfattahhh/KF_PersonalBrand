01

Starting with a student network

By the time I worked with Studypedia, tutoring had already connected me with many students at LAU. People knew me through studying together, asking for help, and finding someone who could explain a difficult topic. That network opened a different kind of opportunity: helping students discover possibilities beyond Lebanon.

As a brand ambassador, I supported Studypedia’s awareness and outreach. The work centered on connecting people with opportunities to explore studying abroad, including options in Europe, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

02

Giving people a reason to attend

A major part of the role was promoting the second International Educational Fair in October 2022. The event took place at the Radisson Blu in Verdun. My contribution involved targeted outreach and personalized communication with prospective attendees.

The value of the event had to feel relevant to the person receiving the invitation. Academic plans, professional ambitions, and uncertainty about the next step all gave those conversations a purpose. I worked to communicate why attending could help students explore their options.

A note I’m carrying forward

Trust makes an invitation worth considering. Follow-through makes it meaningful.

03

From outreach to attendance

I helped bring more than fifty students to the fair. The conversations turned into people physically showing up to explore opportunities, ask questions, and consider a path they might not have taken otherwise.

That was a tangible result for me. It connected the social skills I had developed at university with work that mattered to an organization. It also showed me that the responsibility continues after getting someone’s attention: they are trusting your recommendation with their time.

04

Working with the team

The role involved maintaining a positive, professional relationship with the Studypedia team as well as reaching students. My LinkedIn description reflects pride in that relationship and an ongoing connection to its educational mission.

The fair photographs capture both sides of the experience: representing the agency and being part of the team around the event. They are reminders of a period when I was discovering how naturally teaching, communication, and business could overlap.

05

What I carried forward

This experience helped me recognize the value of the relationships I was building. Outreach worked through conversations with people, shaped by an understanding of what they were looking for.

That remains relevant to my work in education today. An opportunity can exist on paper, but helping someone take the first step toward it takes attention, clear communication, and trust.
